Exploring Khmer Culture

The Khmer culture, originating from Cambodia, is rich in history and tradition.

Key Aspects of Khmer Culture

  • Traditional Cuisine
  • Festivals and Celebrations
  • Art and Architecture
    1. Angkor Wat - A maginficent template complex and a symbol of Khmer Architectural brilliance.
    2. Khmer New Year - A major festival marking the start of the new year with the traditional dances and ceremonies.
    3. Traditional Dance - Includes classical performances such as the Apsara dance, which is deeply rooted in Khmer tradition.

To learn more about Khmer culture, you can visit the Wikipedia page on Khmer culture for an in-depth overview.
